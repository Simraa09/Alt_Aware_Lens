1. Purpose of the Platform

AltAware Lens is designed to help users review the quality and usefulness of alternative text (alt text) associated with images. It may use AI-assisted analysis to identify potential issues and provide suggestions for improvement.

2. AI-Generated Suggestions

AI-generated descriptions and recommendations may be inaccurate, incomplete, misleading, or unsuitable for the context in which an image appears. Users should review suggestions and make appropriate corrections before using them.

3. Accessibility Limitations

Alt text is only one component of digital accessibility. A review performed by AltAware Lens does not constitute a complete accessibility audit and does not guarantee that a website or image complies with WCAG or any other accessibility standard.

4. Scan Results

Results may be affected by website permissions, network failures, dynamically loaded content, or technical limitations. An image or issue that is not detected should not be assumed to be accessible or error-free.

5. No Professional or Legal Advice

The platform provides informational and educational assistance. Its output is not legal advice, a formal accessibility certification, or a substitute for professional evaluation and human judgment.

6. User Responsibility

Users are responsible for checking generated suggestions and ensuring that final image descriptions accurately communicate the relevant information to their intended audience.
