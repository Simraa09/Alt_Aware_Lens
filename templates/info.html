About Alt Aware Lens

Making the web more accessible, one image at a time.

Alt Aware Lens aims to raise awareness about image accessibility and the importance of alternative text for images on websites.

Alternative text, commonly known as alt text, provides a textual description of informative images for people using screen readers and other assistive technologies. Without appropriate alt text, important visual information may be inaccessible to some users.

Our platform focuses on identifying potential alt-text issues in webpage content and helping users understand where improvements may be needed.

Our Purpose

We encourage developers, designers, students, and content creators to consider accessibility while building and maintaining websites.

Alt Aware Lens supports awareness and evaluation. Its findings are intended to guide improvements and do not replace a comprehensive accessibility audit.

How to Help Us

Every step towards better image accessibility matters.

Identify Potential Issues

Help identify webpages where informative images have missing, empty, or potentially unhelpful alt text. When reviewing a page, focus on its main content rather than its header and footer.

Share Your Findings

Share examples of accessibility issues along with the webpage URL and a short explanation of the problem. Avoid including personal or sensitive data.

Write Meaningful Alt Text

If you manage a website, provide concise descriptions that communicate the purpose and relevant information of informative images in their context.

Spread Awareness

Encourage fellow students, developers, designers, and content creators to consider image accessibility when creating digital content.

Remember

Not every image needs a descriptive alt text. Informative images generally need an appropriate description, while purely decorative images usually use an empty alt attribute. The correct approach depends on the image's purpose and context.

AltSight provides AI-assisted suggestions for improving image alternative text. Results may be inaccurate and require human review. AltSight does not certify accessibility or legal compliance.
